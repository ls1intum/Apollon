# Apollon support

Apollon is free, open-source software maintained by the Applied Education
Technologies team at the Technical University of Munich.

## Contact us

For help, bug reports, or feature requests:

- Email [ls1.admin@in.tum.de](mailto:ls1.admin@in.tum.de).
- [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/ls1intum/Apollon/issues/new/choose).

Please include the Apollon version, your device model, operating-system version,
what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is useful when it does not
contain private diagram content. Do not attach confidential diagrams to a public
GitHub issue.

## Updating from the previous iOS app

Install the new version as an update from the App Store. Do not delete the old
app first: an in-place update preserves its local app storage so Apollon can
import compatible diagrams automatically. Check that your diagrams appear in the
new app before removing any separate backups.

You can also export important diagrams as JSON before updating. JSON files can
be imported again from the Apollon home screen.

## Your diagrams and privacy

Local diagrams remain on your device. They are sent to TUM infrastructure only
when you actively choose to share them. Apollon has no advertising or
third-party analytics. Read the current
[privacy statement](https://apollon.aet.cit.tum.de/privacy) for retention,
collaboration, and data-subject information.

For a shared diagram deletion request, email the support address with its share
link or diagram ID. Do not post the link in a public issue.
