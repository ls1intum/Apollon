Assess a diagram without fighting the canvas. Relationships open their feedback
from a single click anywhere along the drawn route, including automatically
routed ones, and nodes no longer shift by a pixel as they highlight. The
feedback popover keeps its navigation in a footer that stays visible while the
form scrolls, so moving to the previous or next assessment — by button or by
arrow key — never scrolls the action out of reach or drops keyboard focus.
Connection handles stay hidden while assessing, since there is nothing to
connect.
