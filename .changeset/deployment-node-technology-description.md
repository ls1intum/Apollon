Give deployment nodes, components, artifacts and the connections between them a technology and a
description. The technology shows in brackets (`[Kotlin/Ktor]`) and the description as a smaller
line below it — under the name on a box, under the label on a connection — and both are edited
from the element's popover. Deployment dependencies also draw their label now, which they were
storing but never showing. Anything left without these looks exactly as it did before.
